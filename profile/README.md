### Key Features

* **Unified Media Library:** Aggregates photos and videos from local hard drives, external USB storage, network drives (NAS), and mobile devices into a single timeline without duplicating original files unless requested.
* **AI Face Recognition & Smart Albums:** Automatically detects and groups faces across large libraries, alongside rule-based Smart Albums that filter media by camera model, lens, EXIF metadata, keywords, or date.
* **Duplicate & Similar Photo Finder:** Scans libraries to identify exact duplicates and visually similar burst shots to help reclaim storage space.
* **Password-Protected Safe Boxes:** Encrypted private vaults inside the library to hide and lock sensitive photo albums behind a password.
* **RAW & Video Support:** Built-in viewing and metadata indexing for major camera RAW formats (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RW2) as well as standard JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and MP4/MOV video files.
